## Product overview

**VK Account Registrator** is a commercial solution for automating permitted browser workflows involving VK accounts that the customer owns or is authorised to administer. It is intended for controlled operational workflows that need organised service data, automation profiles, and an auditable process history.

It works through browser automation. This is **not an official VK product** and does not imply partnership, endorsement, or integration by VK.

## Capabilities

- Browser-based workflows for operations involving authorised accounts.
- Controlled organisation of account-related operational data.
- ZennoPoster profile support for permitted automation scenarios.
- Configurable workflows for content publishing, comment handling, content uploads, and communications, subject to platform rules and applicable law.
- Adaptation to an agreed business workflow.
